Take the photo strip together, wherever you are.

SYNC SHOT is a real-time photobooth. Pick a frame, set the timer, add a filter, and raise your palm to shoot.

Camera access

SYNC SHOT needs your camera to show the live preview and take your photos. Video stays on your device. It is only shared with your partner inside a Sync room.

Why make an account? Your photos stay saved to your account after the session ends, and you can use Sync rooms. Skip and click works without one, but photos are gone when the page reloads.
